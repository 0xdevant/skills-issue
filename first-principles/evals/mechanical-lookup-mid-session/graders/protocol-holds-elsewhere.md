The queue-versus-cron half is a judgment question, so the protocol still governs it.

PASSES: auditing it. Naming a premise inside the question, pointing out that their stated evidence
does not bear on the choice they made, or asking about the part they filed as assumed. A response
of the right shape, on a different problem:

> You filed "the data is the same for everyone" under verified, and it is the premise the whole
> question rests on. Same for everyone, or same for everyone you have tested with?

FAILS only if the response resolves the choice for them:

- says which to pick, or which the evidence points to
- concludes that one of the two is right, even tentatively
- proposes a design that settles it ("run a cron that drains a queue")
