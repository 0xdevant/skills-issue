# Short-form posts

Threads, Instagram captions, Facebook. Short, read on a phone.

**Scope warning — read this before adding to the file.** What belongs here is *language*: register
for short text, and the vocabulary and tone failures specific to writing briefly in Cantonese.

What does **not** belong here, and was removed on 2026-09-09 after being duplicated from a content
skill: post shape, hooks, openings, structure, what makes a post engaging. That is content craft —
house style — and it belongs to whatever content skill or persona drives this one. Putting it here
contradicts the skill's own scope, and the copy immediately started drifting from the original.

If you find yourself writing "a good post opens with…", stop: that's the caller's business.

## Register

Most brand and explainer accounts sit at **L2**, personality accounts at **L3**. See
`register.md` for the dial.

Short-form raises the stakes on the two tests there. Posts get screenshotted and travel without
context, so a line that reads fine inside a thread can land very differently alone. Apply the
**reshare test** harder here than anywhere else.

## The 「最X係」 pivot

Short form is where this construction earns its keep — it forces you to name the single most
striking fact rather than listing several. Full treatment in `register.md`; the filler you pick
sets the register of the whole post.

## Tone failures specific to short Cantonese

- **PSA vocabulary.** 「提高警惕」「注意」「切勿」 read as a government notice, not a person talking.
  This is a vocabulary problem, not a structural one — the words themselves carry the institutional
  register regardless of how the sentence is built.
- **Lecturing constructions.** 「大家要記住…」「我哋應該…」 position you above the reader. Cantonese
  has plenty of ways to say something is worth knowing without instructing anyone.
- **Over-compression.** Cantonese compresses well and short formats tempt you to keep squeezing.
  Past a point the reader has to decompress it, which is slower than reading a slightly longer
  line. See `pitfalls.md`.
- **Explaining the joke.** If a line lands, stop. Adding the explanation is the most common way a
  good Cantonese line is spoiled.

## Length

This skill sets **no** character limit — that's platform and house style. `max_chars` exists in the
lint profiles and defaults to `0` (off) precisely so the caller can set it without the language
layer having an opinion.
