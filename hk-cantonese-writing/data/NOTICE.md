# Bundled dictionary data: sources and licences

These files are generated by `scripts/fetch-dictionaries.mjs` from two upstream sources. Both are
redistributable. Neither is the words.hk *full dictionary*, which is **not** bundled here (see
below).

## `words-hk.txt` (58,004 entries)

Headwords from the **粵典 words.hk word list**, <https://words.hk/faiman/analysis/wordslist/>.

**Licence: public domain.** The source page states: *"Data License: public domain. Credits to
words.hk appreciated."* / 「授權：公有領域。」

Credit to **粵典 words.hk**, <https://words.hk/>.

Headwords only. Definitions, usage notes and register labels are **not** here: those live in the
full dictionary export, which is under the Non-Commercial Open Data License 1.0 and therefore
cannot ship inside a GPL skill. See `references/dictionaries.md`.

## `swc-headwords.txt` (104,867 entries)

Traditional-character headwords, 2 to 4 characters, from **CC-CEDICT**,
<https://www.mdbg.net/chinese/dictionary?page=cedict>.

**Licence: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International (CC BY-SA 4.0)**,
<https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/>. CC BY-SA 4.0 is one-way compatible with
GPL-3.0-or-later, so it may be redistributed here under this skill's licence with attribution.

Credit to the **CC-CEDICT** project and its contributors.

Headwords only, no definitions or pinyin. This list is used as a *negative* signal: a word that is
absent from 粵典 but present here is likely 書面語 that has leaked into Cantonese text.

## Refreshing

```bash
node scripts/fetch-dictionaries.mjs
```
